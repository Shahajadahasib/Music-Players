[Company name] ("we") makes the Aurora Music app. This policy explains what
happens to your information when you use it.

## Summary
- No account is needed. We don't run advertising or analytics in the app.
- Your library (favourites, playlists, listening history, settings) is stored
  **only on your device**.
- To find and play music, the app contacts third-party music services directly.

## Information processed on your device
Favourites, playlists, listening history (up to 500 entries), recent searches,
settings, playback diagnostics, and music files you choose to import. None of
this is sent to us. You can erase it at any time in **Settings → Delete all my
data**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Third-party music services
When you search or play, your device sends the search text and requests audio
from the enabled services. Those services also receive your IP address. The
services are:
- Audius (audius.co): search and streaming
- Internet Archive (archive.org): search and streaming
- Apple iTunes Search API (apple.com): song information

Their own privacy policies apply. You can turn services off in
**Settings → Music sources**.

## Purchases
- **App Store / Google Play:** Apple or Google processes your payment. We don't
  receive your payment details. If server verification is enabled, the app sends
  the store's purchase token / transaction id to our licence server
  ([licence server URL]) only to confirm the purchase is genuine.
- **Web:** Stripe processes your payment. Our licence server stores the checkout
  session id, a licence id, and a one-way hash of your e-mail address, so we can
  re-issue your key. We keep these for [retention period, e.g. 7 years for
  tax records].
- Your licence is stored in your device's secure storage.
